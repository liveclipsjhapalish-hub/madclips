Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Summary

Mad Clips Uploader is a personal tool used only by its operator. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone else.

2. Data the App uses

  • Login tokens issued by the platforms (for example TikTok Login Kit) after the operator approves access. They are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used only to publish the operator's posts.
  • Basic account info of the operator's own account (display name and avatar) to show which account a post goes to.
  • The video files and captions the operator chooses to post.

3. What the App does not do

It does not read, store or analyse other users' data, followers, messages or analytics. It has no advertising or tracking.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to the platform being posted to (for example TikTok), as needed to publish the post.

5. Deleting data

The operator can revoke the App's access at any time in the platform's settings (for example TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services) and delete the stored tokens from the computer.
